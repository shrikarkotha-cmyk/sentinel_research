# Personal research journal

The researcher should write entries in their own words after each session. Do not backdate or invent entries.

## Entry template
- Date and time spent:
- What I wanted to learn:
- What I personally did:
- What AI or other sources helped with:
- What worked or failed:
- Why I think it happened:
- What I can explain independently:
- Next experiment:

Before posting, remove personal details, network addresses, identifiers, private keys, and activation codes. Maintain any sensitive raw notes separately.
