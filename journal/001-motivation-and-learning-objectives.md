**Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Topic:** Motivation and Learning Objectives

## Why I Started Sentinel

I noticed how frustrated people were with existing anti-piracy systems, particularly Denuvo. It made me wonder why there wasn't a better solution that could protect games without negatively affecting legitimate players.

As a gamer who has personally experienced problems with Denuvo, I wanted to investigate whether an alternative approach could provide effective protection while being easier for players to use.

## What I Hope to Learn

Through Sentinel, I hope to develop a deeper understanding of software infrastructure, cybersecurity, activation servers, TPM technology, and cryptographic licensing.

Even if Sentinel does not become a commercially successful product, I want to understand the challenges behind anti-piracy technology and gain practical experience designing, building, and testing security systems.

## Central Objective

My goal is to explore whether game security and a positive player experience can coexist without significant compromises.
