# V0.18 security and performance candidate: evidence intake

Status: recommended development baseline, not a commercial release. V0.17.2 is retained for comparison and rollback.

The supplied archive contains 111 entries. Intake verified its CRCs, its reported SHA-256 (f343ee1aa4d0fd2e1bfd4eeb459e9754693612bb174d3a40ee5eb0124cfe5615), all 110 file hashes in SHA256SUMS, and the embedded V0.17.2 baseline checksum. No conventional private-key, license or database file extensions were found. These are archive consistency checks, not independent reproduction of the Windows results.

## Reported scalability and correctness
The SQLite candidate uses a bounded grouped-commit writer. Source inspection confirms that it completes the database transaction before resolving successful responses. In the matched 16-worker synthetic workload, 50,000 arrivals took 133.359 seconds on V0.17.2 and 6.941 seconds on V0.18 (374.93 versus 7203.48 requests/s). The candidate recorded 6251 transactions for 45000 writer operations, largest group 15. Both variants produced 40000 independently checked licenses and 10000 expected denials, zero incorrect decisions and successful database integrity checks. A separate replay reported 6.98 seconds. PostgreSQL prototypes took 36–41 seconds in their own local comparisons. Four-worker SQLite trials missed the ten-second goal. Queue p95 in the matched 16-worker candidate was 6.45 seconds. These are in-process arrival simulations, not public-network capacity or multi-host tests. Power-loss recovery and response-loss behavior require additional qualification.

## Reported security and compatibility
The extracted archive rebuild records 27 successful qualification steps, including 76/76 player cases. The report also records 12/12 audit, 16/16 recovery, 9/9 bound grant and 6/6 new defensive-control cases. Planned issuer overlap, retired issuer rejection on updated builds, encrypted backup/restore and stale-SDK packaging rejection were exercised. Older disconnected builds still trust their original issuer. An initial retirement trial failed and its partial evidence was retained. V0.17.2 licenses worked with TPM and DPAPI on the tested Windows host. No new physical platform result was independently observed during intake.

## Performance and Adaptive
Reported median first-frame time was 355.6 ms protected versus 259.0 ms renderer-only control. The control omits full game logic; this is not a commercial DRM overhead measurement. Presented FPS was about 60, while backbuffer throughput varied by preset. Reported presented 1% lows were lower in the protected fixture. Additional matched repetitions and full-game controls are needed.
Adaptive is developer-side bounded incident intake, registered reproduction, regression evidence collection and a human review gate. It does not generate patches autonomously. No update was approved or distributed. Its security assumes trusted build inputs, operators and signing custody.
