# Research integrity and contributions

The project is an independent student learning and research project. AI assistance has contributed to prototype code, research discovery, drafting, editing and planning. The student supplied the project motivation, ran guided Windows and macOS experiments, reported results, and participated in design decisions. Contributions will be recorded as development proceeds; no independent authorship or unaided work is implied.

The student should review sources directly, understand and explain implementation choices, and maintain their own dated journal. For a school, competition, or publication, follow its specific disclosure and eligibility policies. Report failed tests and uncertainty, distinguish planned features from demonstrated results, and never invent observations. All test targets must be owned or explicitly authorized. Public documentation must exclude credentials and identifying raw logs.
